# Sample engagement — fictional

**Everything in this folder is invented.** Bayline Fitness does not exist. The people, numbers, and findings are written for this showcase to demonstrate the engagement shape. No client material appears here.

## The brief

- **Client (fictional):** Bayline Fitness, a three-location fitness chain.
- **Question:** Should we open a fourth location, and what would it take to run it without the owner on site every day?
- **Scope:** Demand and cost for one candidate neighborhood; the operating system a fourth location would need; a go / no-go recommendation.
- **Done means:** a written diagnostic with a recommendation, the evidence behind it, and the open items.

## Findings (abridged)

1. Demand in the candidate neighborhood supports a fourth location at the chain's current price point — based on the chain's own member data and local demographics, both cited in the full diagnostic.
2. The chain's current operating system does not survive a fourth location: scheduling, onboarding, and incident handling all depend on the owner personally. This is the binding constraint, not demand.
3. The gap is closable: documented checklists, a named deputy per location, and a weekly operating review would cover it, at a cost the diagnostic estimates.

## Recommendation

**Conditional go.** Open the fourth location only after the operating system in finding 2 is documented and deputized at the existing three locations. Opening first and systematizing later repeats the exact failure the diagnostic found.

## Claims (abridged — see `claims.tsv`)

| # | Claim | Evidence | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Demand supports a fourth location at current pricing | Member data + local demographics, cited in diagnostic | Supported |
| 2 | Current operations depend on the owner personally | Staff interviews + schedule records, cited in diagnostic | Supported |
| 3 | The gap is closable before opening | Comparable chains' operating checklists + cost estimate | Supported, estimate only |

## Close

- **Decided:** conditional go, with the operating-system work first.
- **Delivered:** this diagnostic.
- **Open items:** deputy hiring (owner's decision); lease terms for the candidate site (not in scope).
